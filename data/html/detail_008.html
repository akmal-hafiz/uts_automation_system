Senior Software Automation Quality Assurance

PT Infokes Indonesia

Rekruter aktif 2j lalu
  • Penuh waktu
  • On-site • Bandung
    • Min. 3-5 Years Experience
  • Tidak Ditampilkan
Rekruter aktif 2j lalu

Deskripsi Pekerjaan

  1. Work with Product Managers, Dev team, and UI/UX team in understanding the product, domain, and various features.
  2. Review quality specifications and technical design documents to provide timely and meaningful feedback.
  3. Create detailed, comprehensive, and well-structured test plans and test cases/scenario ensuring high-quality software delivery.
  4. Estimate and prioritize testing activities.
  5. Collaborate with cross-functional teams to ensure the highest level of product quality.
  6. Conduct quality assurance and troubleshoot any errors.
  7. Analyze complex software systems and collaborate with others to improve the overall design, testability, and quality.
  8. Ensure all tests and procedures meet company and industry standards.
  9. Report, track, and monitor defects/bugs.

Kualifikasi

  1. Strong knowledge of software QA methodologies, tools, and processes.
  2. Good analytical thinking (analyze logs and API responses, analyze bugs)
  3. Strong attention to detail and problem-solving skills.
  4. Excellent communication and team collaboration skills.
  5. Experience with Agile methodologies, including sprints and retrospective.
  6. Experience with Automation testing
  7. Proficiency in testing Web Apps, Mobile Apps (iOS & Android apps), and API
  8. Familiarity with testing tools such as QASEio, Postman
  9. Familiarity with automation tools such Playwright, K6 is a plus
  10. Understanding of database concepts and queries (MySQL) is a plus.
  11. Experience with performance and/or security testing is a plus.
  12. Experience in development software process, preferably in Health sectors is a plus.
  13. Able to work from office (WFO Bandung)
